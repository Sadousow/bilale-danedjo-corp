feat: Meta catalog selection, social links section, and shop domain attachment

Three connected pieces of work, plus the fixes and guards they surfaced.

Meta catalog — partial publication
  Merchants pick which products go to their Meta catalog rather than pushing
  everything. New MetaCatalogItem model (added to TENANT_SCOPED), hand-written
  migration, and an eligibility check that refuses locally — with a reason in
  French — instead of letting Meta reject silently. The dangerous state is a
  product published to Meta but deactivated in the shop: the ad runs and leads
  to an empty page, with nothing failing anywhere. That state is now flagged.
  Nothing is sent to Meta yet; the connection does not exist.

Social links — dedicated section
  Moved out of Appearance into /admin/reseaux-sociaux, with validation that was
  missing: "@handle" became "https://@handle", a dead link shown in the footer,
  and a Facebook URL pasted into the Instagram field went unnoticed.

Shop domains — automatic attachment
  A wildcard DNS record makes <slug>.guygou.com resolve, but Vercel only serves
  a certificate for hostnames declared on the project. Without that call, a new
  shop resolves and refuses the connection. Provisioning now declares it after
  the transaction, never inside it, and never fails signup over it.

Fixes
  findUnique on sku/phone stopped working when uniqueness became composite with
  tenantId. Three occurrences, including the POS path that attaches a customer
  to a sale. tsc had flagged them; ignoreBuildErrors let them ship.

Guards
  verif:requetes detects both documented blind spots of the tenant guard —
  findUnique on a composite field, and raw SQL without tenantId — deriving the
  field list from the schema itself.
  Migration history is not replayable: mig2 and plateforme both add
  Settings.slogan, and migrate dev replays alphabetically. diagnostic-migrations
  and reparer-mig2 document and fix it. Use db:migrate:deploy meanwhile.

New test suites, all pure logic, no database:
  test:meta (58), test:reseaux (41), test:domaines (23)
